# MyChats 💬⚡ — Your Private, Real-Time Second Brain

> **Post it on your PC. Read it on your phone. Ask AI anything. Turn messy thoughts into ready-to-run tasks.**
> MyChats is your lightning-fast private workspace for live messaging, AI assistants, and organized tasks — synced instantly across every device you own.

---

## Why You'll Love MyChats

Imagine a single place where your links, notes, screenshots, AI prompts, and to-dos all live — always in sync, always private, always instant. No heavy apps. No noisy group chats. No lost copy-paste.

- ⚡ **Instant everywhere** — type on your laptop, see it on your phone in under a second
- 🤖 **AI built in** — chat with AI, build your own AI tools, translate Arabic ideas into perfect English prompts
- ✅ **From chaos to tasks** — paste messy thoughts, get structured tasks with priorities, subtasks, and even ready-to-run agent prompts
- 🔒 **Private by design** — your archives and accounts stay behind login; live chat stays fast and open for you
- 🎨 **Beautiful anywhere** — gorgeous dark & light themes, flawless on mobile and desktop

---

## 🌐 Global Live Chat — Your Cross-Device Clipboard on Steroids

Your always-on shared room across all devices.

**What you get:** send text, links, and images from any device and watch them appear everywhere in real time. Every message shows who sent it and from which device (`💻 Work Laptop`, `📱 Phone`).

**Why it matters:** kill the "email it to myself" habit forever. Paste a link on your desktop, open your phone, and it's already there.

---

## 🤖 AI Mode + Custom AI Tools Studio — Your Personal AI Team

Flip any conversation from **Chat Mode** to **AI Mode** with one click (or just press `Tab`).

**What you get:**
- Chat directly with AI powered by `llama.cpp`
- Build unlimited custom AI assistants — SQL Generator, Code Reviewer, Business Writer, anything — each with its own name, icon, description, and system prompt
- Deep-link tools with `Open in Chat`, pin favorites, and keep every tool conversation **private to the device you're on**

**Why it matters:** stop re-explaining yourself to generic chatbots. Create the perfect assistant once, reuse it forever.

---

## 🌍 Technical Translator — From Arabic Thoughts to Perfect AI Prompts

Type in Arabic (even Arabizi), get back a clean, precise, technical English prompt — ready to paste into any AI coding agent.

**Why it matters:** if you think in Arabic but build in English, this is your superpower. Your dialect becomes industry-grade instructions with variables, code, and formatting perfectly preserved.

---

## ✅ My Tasks — Messy Notes In, Organized Action Out

Your shared, real-time task list with an AI brain.

**What you get:**
- **AI Task Formatter:** paste *"deploy backend, fix mobile navbar before 5pm urgent, send docs to team"* — get back titled tasks with priorities, tags, subtasks, and due dates
- **Agent Execution Prompts:** for technical tasks, AI writes a complete, self-contained handoff prompt (objective + context + step-by-step + verification) you can copy straight into Claude, ChatGPT, or Antigravity
- **Convert to Task (AI):** hover any chat message → one click turns it into tasks
- Priorities, tags, due dates, subtasks with checklists, manual tasks, filters, live sync, and one-click `Clear Done`

**Why it matters:** your chat is no longer a graveyard of good intentions. Every idea becomes trackable, assignable action in seconds.

## 📝 Rich Messages — Code, Markdown, Images, Links

- Beautiful **Markdown**: headings, tables, quotes, lists
- **Code blocks** with language badges and one-click copy

## 📡 Real-Time Magic — Powered by SSE

No refresh. No spinning. Server-Sent Events push every message, delete, pin, archive, and task update to all your screens instantly — with automatic reconnection, heartbeats, and low battery use.

---

## 🗄️ Admin Archive — Your Memory, Searchable Forever

Hit **Clear & Archive** and your live room resets everywhere while the full history is saved — auto-titled from its first line.

Search archives, preview full conversations, rename, pin important ones to the top, export to **Markdown or JSON**, restore, or delete. Plus live stats: total archives, messages, and live activity.

## 👥 Accounts & Devices — Made for Your Whole Setup

Create device accounts (Work Laptop, Phone, Home Desktop), tag them with emoji labels, assign Admin/User roles, activate or deactivate access, and see every message attributed to its source. Safety guards protect your own account and your last admin.

2. Name your devices (`💻 Work Laptop`, `📱 Phone`)
3. Post your first link — watch it appear on the other screen
4. Press `Tab`, ask AI anything
5. Paste messy notes into My Tasks → hit **AI Structure**
